# Paper links

# <a id="papers"></a>Papers

The notes link these works where their ideas arise. This index groups selected references by their mathematical role.

| Work | Idea developed in the module |
| --- | --- |
| LeCun, Bottou, Bengio, and Haffner, [*Gradient-Based Learning Applied to Document Recognition*](https://bottou.org/papers/lecun-98h) | Convolutional networks for handwritten digits, and a comparison of linear, nearest-neighbor, kernel, and network classifiers on MNIST. |
| DeCoste and Schölkopf, [*Training Invariant Support Vector Machines*](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1012454411458) | Building knowledge of image transformations into a kernel classifier. |
| Cireşan, Meier, and Schmidhuber, [*Multi-column Deep Neural Networks for Image Classification*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.2745) | Committees of convolutional networks trained on distorted digits. |
| Krizhevsky, Sutskever, and Hinton, [*ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks*](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2012/hash/c399862d3b9d6b76c8436e924a68c45b-Abstract.html) | Learned features outperforming hand-designed image features on a large benchmark. |
| Xie, Girshick, and Farhadi, [*Unsupervised Deep Embedding for Clustering Analysis*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06335) | Clustering accuracy of k-means on raw MNIST pixels, compared with clustering in a learned representation. |
| Azzalini and Bowman, [*A Look at Some Data on the Old Faithful Geyser*](https://doi.org/10.2307/2347385) | The geyser's waiting times as an example of density and conditional density estimation. |
| Baydin, Pearlmutter, Radul, and Siskind, [*Automatic Differentiation in Machine Learning: a Survey*](https://jmlr.org/papers/v18/17-468.html) | Forward and reverse differentiation, derivative products, and computational cost. |
| Pearlmutter, [*Fast Exact Multiplication by the Hessian*](https://doi.org/10.1162/neco.1994.6.1.147) | Hessian–vector products from forward-mode differentiation of a gradient. |
| Franceschi, Donini, Frasconi, and Pontil, [*Forward and Reverse Gradient-Based Hyperparameter Optimization*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.01785) | Forward-mode derivatives with respect to a few hyperparameters of a long training run. |
| Duchi, Hazan, and Singer, [*Adaptive Subgradient Methods for Online Learning and Stochastic Optimization*](https://jmlr.org/papers/v12/duchi11a.html) | Accumulated coordinate scaling and adaptive optimization. |
| Kingma and Ba, [*Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6980) | Moving gradient moments and bias correction. |
| Goh, [*Why Momentum Really Works*](https://distill.pub/2017/momentum/) | Gradient descent and momentum on quadratics, analyzed one eigendirection at a time; the source of four figures in the calculus chapter. |
| Shannon, [*A Mathematical Theory of Communication*](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x) | Entropy, coding, and communication. |
| Valiant, [*A Theory of the Learnable*](https://doi.org/10.1145/1968.1972) | A computational formulation of learnability. |
| Vapnik and Chervonenkis, [*On the Uniform Convergence of Relative Frequencies of Events to Their Probabilities*](https://doi.org/10.1137/1116025) | Uniform convergence and class capacity. |
| Bartlett and Mendelson, [*Rademacher and Gaussian Complexities: Risk Bounds and Structural Results*](https://jmlr.org/papers/v3/bartlett02a.html) | Complexity measures for generalization. |
| Bousquet and Elisseeff, [*Stability and Generalization*](https://jmlr.org/papers/v2/bousquet02a.html) | How the sensitivity of an algorithm controls generalization. |
| Seeger, [*PAC-Bayesian Generalisation Error Bounds for Gaussian Process Classification*](https://jmlr.org/papers/v3/seeger02a.html) | Generalization bounds for distributions over predictors. |
| Blanchard, Higham, and Higham, [*Accurate Computation of the Log-Sum-Exp and Softmax Functions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03469) | Rounding-error analysis of stable probability calculations. |
